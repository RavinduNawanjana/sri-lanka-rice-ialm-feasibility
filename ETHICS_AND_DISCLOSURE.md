# Ethics, Disclosure, and Claim Boundaries

This repository is an analytical research portfolio constructed from project materials supplied by the researcher. It is not an independent replication performed by a third party.

The author had a professional role connected to the underlying feasibility project. That relationship is relevant context for readers evaluating provenance and potential conflicts of interest. The repository therefore emphasizes reproducibility, explicit source disagreements, public-safe data release, and separation of observed results from project claims.

No claim is made that the field trial produced verified credits, satisfied Verra additionality, completed validation/verification, or established the causal efficacy of every practice. Those questions require evidence beyond the files currently available.
