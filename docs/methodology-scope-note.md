# Methodology Scope Note: VM0042 and VM0051

## Why this matters

The field work occurred in 2025 while the study documents were framed around VM0042. The Verra methodology landscape subsequently changed in a way that is directly relevant to flooded rice.

## VM0042

VM0042 v2.2 is an active improved agricultural land-management methodology covering, among other practices, reduced tillage, fertilizer management, residue management, and water management. It has broad agricultural applicability and combines project-level baseline, additionality, quantification, leakage, uncertainty, and monitoring requirements.

Official page: https://verra.org/methodologies/vm0042-improved-agricultural-land-management-v2-2/

## VM0051

Verra lists **VM0051 Improved Management in Rice Production Systems v1.1** as active since 14 July 2026. It applies specifically to improved water and crop-management practices in flooded rice systems to reduce GHG emissions. VM0051 v1.0, first active in February 2025, is scheduled for inactivation in August 2027 subject to Verra's transition rules.

Official page: https://verra.org/methodologies/vm0051-improved-management-in-rice-production-systems-v1-1/

## What this repository concludes

It would be historically inaccurate to rewrite the 2025 study as if it had originally been designed under the 2026 revision of VM0051. It would also be methodologically careless to ignore the existence of a rice-specific methodology when discussing future project development.

Accordingly:

- the repository describes the 2025 study under its documented VM0042 framing;
- it treats current methodology selection as a separate project-design decision;
- it does not assert that the trial satisfies the applicability conditions of VM0042 or VM0051;
- it does not infer the optimal methodology from the 32-row agronomic dataset.

Any future carbon-credit project should perform a fresh applicability and methodology-selection review using the active Verra documents at the time of submission.
