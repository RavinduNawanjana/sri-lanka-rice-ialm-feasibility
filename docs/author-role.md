# Researcher Role and Contribution Statement

## Role in the underlying project

During the 2025 feasibility work, the portfolio author served as Head of Impact Investments and Sustainability for the private-sector project partner. The broader role included carbon-program design, methodology and additionality work, preparation of technical/financial documentation, and coordination with public/research stakeholders.

This description should not be read as a claim that the author personally performed every agronomic measurement, chamber sampling event, laboratory analysis, or field operation. The project source package reflects work by multiple institutional and field contributors.

## Contribution to this public research repository

The public reconstruction contributes:

- cross-source recovery of the experimental coding structure;
- treatment-definition audit and conservative public codebook;
- data cleaning and tidy public data structures;
- experimental-unit and pseudoreplication diagnosis;
- reproducible Python analysis plus R/Stata equivalents;
- data-quality and claims registers;
- current VM0042/VM0051 methodology crosswalk;
- privacy/proprietary-information sanitization;
- a staged analysis plan for future GHG measurements.

## Authorship boundary

The analytical narrative, code, figures, and public documentation in this repository are a research-portfolio product. Original institutional reports, field measurements, photographs, and proprietary schedules remain source materials with their own provenance and ownership.
